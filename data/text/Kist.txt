- Hospitality and tourism
- Entrepreneurship
- Stock market / securities

KIST also states that its BBA programme includes practical management training and supports placement, entrepreneurship, industry interaction and research/development activities.

Admission:
BBA applicants generally need:
- Minimum D in theory and C in practical in each Grade 11/12 subject with CGPA 1.8 or above; OR
- Minimum second division in 10+2, PCL or equivalent in any discipline.
- CMAT is required for the admission process.
- KIST admission form and personal interview are also part of the process.
